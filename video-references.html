⚠️ Red Flags - Videos to Avoid

Be Cautious of Videos That:

  • Promise miracle cures or "one food that cures everything"
  • Advise stopping prescribed medications
  • Promote expensive supplements without evidence
  • Use fear tactics or extreme claims
  • Are from unverified sources without credentials
  • Contradict basic medical science
  • Sell products directly
  • Share "secrets doctors don't want you to know"

Signs of Reliable Content

  • Made by healthcare professionals with verifiable credentials
  • From recognized medical institutions
  • Cites sources and research
  • Presents balanced information including limitations
  • Encourages consulting healthcare providers
  • Avoids sensational claims
  • Updated or reviewed recently

💡 Tips for Using Video Resources

Getting the Most from Videos

  • Take notes: Write down questions to ask your doctor
  • Verify information: Cross-check with other reliable sources
  • Check dates: Medical information changes; prefer recent videos
  • Use subtitles: Most platforms offer auto-generated captions
  • Save useful videos: Create playlists for reference
  • Share with family: Help caregivers understand your condition

Before Doctor Visits

  • Watch videos about your condition to understand terminology
  • Learn about potential treatments to ask informed questions
  • Watch procedure videos to know what to expect
  • Prepare questions based on what you learned

Accessibility Features

  • Use playback speed controls for complex topics
  • Enable closed captions/subtitles
  • Use pause and rewind for understanding
  • Download videos when allowed for offline viewing
