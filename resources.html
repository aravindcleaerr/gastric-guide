Organizations, tools, and support for digestive health

Disclaimer: These resources are provided for informational purposes. Inclusion does not constitute endorsement. Always verify information and consult healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

🇮🇳 Indian Organizations and Resources

Professional Medical Organizations

  • Indian Society of Gastroenterology (ISG) - Premier GI professional body
  • Indian National Association for Study of the Liver (INASL) - Liver disease focus
  • Association of Surgeons of India (ASI) - Surgical gastroenterology section
  • Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) - Pediatric GI resources
  • Digestive Diseases Foundation of India - Public education

Government Health Resources

  • National Health Portal (nhp.gov.in) - Health information in multiple languages
  • Ayushman Bharat (pmjay.gov.in) - Government health insurance scheme
  • eSanjeevani - Free government telemedicine service
  • National Health Mission - State health programs
  • Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) - Food safety information

Patient Support Organizations

  • Indian IBD Foundation - Support for Crohn's and ulcerative colitis patients
  • Celiac Support India (CELFIN) - Celiac disease support and resources
  • Liver Foundation West Bengal - Liver disease awareness and support
  • Cancer Patients Aid Association - Support for GI cancer patients
  • Ostomy Association of India - Support for ostomy patients

🔬 Research and Guidelines

Finding Reliable Medical Information

  • PubMed (pubmed.gov) - Medical research database
  • Cochrane Library - Systematic reviews of medical evidence
  • UpToDate - Professional medical resource (subscription)
  • BMJ Best Practice - Clinical decision support

Clinical Guidelines

  • Indian Society of Gastroenterology guidelines
  • ACG Clinical Guidelines
  • AGA Guidelines
  • NICE Guidelines (UK)
  • World Gastroenterology Organisation Global Guidelines

Understanding Research

  • Look for systematic reviews and meta-analyses for strongest evidence
  • Check if studies are peer-reviewed
  • Note study size and methodology
  • Consider if findings apply to you (population studied)
  • Discuss research findings with your doctor

💊 Medication Resources

Drug Information

  • CDSCO (cdsco.gov.in) - Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (India)
  • Drugs.com - Drug information, interactions
  • Medscape Drug Reference - Professional drug information
  • RxList - Drug information and identifiers

Affordable Medications

  • Jan Aushadhi Stores - Government generic medicine stores (very affordable)
  • AMRIT Pharmacies - Affordable medicines (AIIMS)
  • Generic medicine options - Ask doctors to prescribe by generic name
  • Online pharmacy price comparison - Compare prices across platforms

Drug Interactions

  • Always inform doctors of all medications (including OTC and supplements)
  • Use interaction checkers when starting new medications
  • Pharmacists can check for interactions
  • Keep an updated medication list

🤝 Support Services

Financial Assistance

  • Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) - Government health insurance
  • State health schemes - Aarogyasri, Karunya, etc.
  • Hospital charity programs - Most large hospitals have these
  • NGO assistance - Various organizations help with medical costs
  • Crowdfunding platforms - Milaap, Ketto for medical expenses
  • Prime Minister's National Relief Fund - For serious illness

Mental Health Support

  • Chronic illness affects mental health - seek support
  • Hospital social workers can connect to resources
  • Online support groups for specific conditions
  • Counseling services at major hospitals
  • Helplines for anxiety and depression (see Helplines section)

Caregiver Support

  • Caregiver support groups (hospital-based or online)
  • Respite care services
  • Educational resources for caregivers
  • Mental health support for caregivers
